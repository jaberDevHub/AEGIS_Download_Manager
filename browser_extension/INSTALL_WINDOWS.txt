Aegis Browser Extension - Zero-Setup Installation

Requirements:
- Aegis Download Manager
- Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, or any Chromium browser

Quick Setup:
1. Open your browser and go to your extensions page:
   - Chrome / Brave: chrome://extensions
   - Edge: edge://extensions
2. Enable "Developer mode" (toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select this 'browser_extension' folder.
4. Launch Aegis Download Manager!

That's it!
- No scripts or bat files required.
- The extension automatically connects to Aegis via the high-speed local desktop bridge.
- Open YouTube or any video site — the floating "Download Video" panel will automatically appear on video players with instant quality options (4K, 1080p, 720p, MP3 Audio).
